Privacy Policy for StoragePlus

Last updated: November 2026

StoragePlus ("we", "our", or "app") is developed as an Android utility tool for automated media backup and cloud storage synchronization. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring your personal data remains completely secure.

1. Information We Access

  • Local Media (Photos & Videos): StoragePlus accesses photos and videos stored on your device solely to perform automatic and manual backups to your connected Google Drive account.
  • Google Account Credentials: We use Google OAuth 2.0 authentication. The app requests access to your Google Drive to upload, organize, and retrieve your backed-up files.

2. How We Use Google User Data

  • Direct Cloud Sync: All media files are transferred directly between your device and your personal Google Drive storage.
  • No Third-Party Sharing: We do NOT sell, lease, transfer, or share your personal data, photos, videos, or Google credentials with any third parties or external servers.
  • No AI Training: Your media and personal data are never used to train machine learning or artificial intelligence models.

3. Data Storage and Security

Authentication tokens are stored locally on your device using encrypted Android Keystore storage. StoragePlus does not host private servers storing your media files; all storage resides securely within your Google Drive account.

4. User Rights and Data Revocation

You can disconnect StoragePlus from your Google Drive account at any time via your Google Account Security Settings (Google Account Permissions). Uninstalling the app removes all locally stored authentication data.
